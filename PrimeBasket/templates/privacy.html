Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you create a PrimeBasket account, we collect your name, email address, and phone number. When you place an order, we also collect your delivery address and payment method selection so we can fulfil that order.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, manage your account, respond to support requests, and -- only if you subscribe -- send you occasional offers and updates. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your password is never stored in plain text; it is hashed before being saved. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized systems needed to run your account, cart, wishlist, and orders.

Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you signed in while you browse. This cookie is required for login-protected features like your cart, wishlist, and order history to work.

Your Choices

You can update your profile details or delete your account at any time from your Profile page. Deleting your account permanently removes your saved profile information.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, reach us any time via our Contact page.
